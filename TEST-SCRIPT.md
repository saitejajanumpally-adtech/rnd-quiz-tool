# Test script for teammates (not admins)

Use a normal databeat.io account. Tick each line. Write down anything odd, with a screenshot.

**First sign-in**
- [ ] A welcome tour appears. Start it, go through the six steps, and finish it.
- [ ] Open Menu > Take the tour. It plays again.
- [ ] Sign out from Menu, and sign in again. The tour does NOT appear a second time.

**Arena**
- [ ] The greeting, three scorecards, and the journey are shown. The text in the scorecards is centered. There is no date filter.
- [ ] A mascot picture shows. If you choose Off in Profile > Mascots > Show mascots, the pictures disappear.

**Taking a quiz** (when a test quiz is open)
- [ ] Required questions show a "Required" tag.
- [ ] On the review screen, Submit stays off while a required question is blank. "Go to question" takes you to it.
- [ ] Press Submit. A confirmation box appears. Cancel keeps your answers open.
- [ ] Confirm. A "Round complete" screen appears. Continue leads to "You're in. Answers are locked."

**Results** (after a test quiz is released)
- [ ] Leaderboard shows the Top 10 with arrows, and a "Your round" panel.
- [ ] There is no "View full leaderboard" button for you (only leads and above see it).
- [ ] In the Quiz library, open the quiz. Switch "Show my answers" off: your answers and score disappear, the correct answers stay.

**Account**
- [ ] Profile > Account shows your Google name and has Sign out and Remove my profile.
- [ ] Remove my profile (use a test account): you are signed out, then signing in again brings your history back.
